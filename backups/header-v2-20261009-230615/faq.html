    Frequently Asked Questions

    Answers to the most common questions about admission and the portal

    📋 Application & Admission

    How do I apply for admission?

    Visit the Apply Now page and complete the guided 4-step form. Make sure you have your JAMB registration number, score, and O'Level results ready.

    After submission you'll receive a unique application number (e.g. FPU/ND/2026/00001). Save it — you'll need it to check your status.

    What are the entry requirements for ND?
    • 5 O'Level credits (WAEC / NECO / NABTEB) including English Language and Mathematics
    • JAMB UTME score of 100 and above
    • Chose FPU as first choice or applied for change of institution via JAMB
    • Minimum age of 16 years
    What are the entry requirements for HND?
    • Relevant National Diploma (ND) in a related field
    • Minimum of Lower Credit (CGPA 2.50 or above)
    • Completed one year of Industrial Training (SIWES)
    • 5 O'Level credits including English and Mathematics
    • ND transcript from your previous institution
    Is the application free?

    Yes — the application itself is completely free. There is no application fee. Once your application is approved, you'll pay acceptance and school fees through the student portal.

    🔍 Application Status

    How do I check my application status?

    Go to the Check Status page and enter:

    • Your application number (e.g. FPU/ND/2026/00001)
    • The email address you used to apply

    You'll see the real-time status: Pending, Under Review, Approved, Rejected, or Registered.

    How long does it take to process my application?

    Applications are typically reviewed within 2–4 weeks. You can check the status page anytime to see the latest update. We'll also email you when a decision is made.

    What if my application is rejected?

    If your application doesn't meet the entry requirements, it will be marked as "Not Approved." You'll see the reason on the status page. You may reapply in the next admission cycle if you improve your results.

    🎓 After Approval

    How do I create my student account?

    Once your application is approved, you'll receive a registration link by email. Click it to set your password, or visit the Register page and enter your unique token.

    After creating your account, you'll be assigned a matric number — save it carefully, you'll use it to log in.

    How do I pay my school fees?

    Log in to the student portal, go to the Fees page, and follow the payment instructions. Use the official bank accounts listed on the Bursar's page, then submit your proof of payment for verification.

    How do I register my courses?

    After logging in to the portal, go to Course Registration. Pick your courses from the available list — the system will enforce the maximum unit limit. Once you submit, your registration goes to your department for approval.

    Can I apply for HND after completing ND?

    Yes. If you have an ND with Lower Credit or higher and have completed SIWES, you can apply for HND through the HND application form. Your ND records should be available for verification.

    💻 Portal & Technical

    I forgot my password — what do I do?

    On the login page, click "Forgot password?". You'll receive a reset link by email. If you don't receive it, contact the ICT unit at contact@fedpolyugep.edu.ng.

    How do I upload my passport photograph?

    Log in to the portal and go to My Photo. Upload a clear JPG or PNG file under 5 MB. Square photos work best. Your photo will appear on your student ID card and profile.

    Where is the school located?

    Our main campus is at Km 95 Calabar–Ikom Highway, Ugep, the headquarters of Yakurr Local Government Area, Cross River State, Nigeria.

    Our second campus (agricultural) is nearby, and we have offices at the Ugep–Ediba road campus.

    What is SIWES?

    SIWES stands for Students Industrial Work Experience Scheme — a mandatory industrial attachment programme where ND and HND students gain real work experience at an approved industry placement.

    It's typically one year (12 months) and is required before progressing from ND to HND.
