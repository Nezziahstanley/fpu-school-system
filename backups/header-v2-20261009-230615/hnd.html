    HND Programmes

    Advanced technical training for ND graduates

    Our Higher National Diploma (HND) programmes are advanced, two-year courses designed for ND graduates who want to specialise, lead, and innovate.

    HND graduates are equipped for senior technical roles, entrepreneurship, and further academic progression.

    Before You Apply

    HND Entry Requirements

    📋 Academic Requirements

    • Relevant National Diploma (ND)
    • Minimum of Lower Credit (CGPA 2.50 or above)
    • Completed one year of Industrial Training (SIWES)
    • 5 O'Level credits including English and Mathematics

    📄 Documents Required

    • ND Statement of Result / Certificate
    • Industrial Training (IT) Certificate
    • ND Transcript from your previous institution
    • O'Level Result (WAEC / NECO / NABTEB)
    • Birth Certificate or Age Declaration
    • Recent passport photographs
