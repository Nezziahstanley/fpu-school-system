    Welcome Message

    A Library Fit for the Digital Age

    Dr Romanus Okechukwu Attama serves as the Polytechnic Librarian at Federal Polytechnic Ugep, leading a team dedicated to supporting the academic and research needs of students, staff, and the wider academic community.

    Our library houses over 10,000 volumes spanning engineering, science, business, and the humanities, with a growing collection of digital resources. We continue to expand our physical and digital collections to serve the evolving needs of modern learners.

    Students can search the catalogue, reserve books, and manage their borrows directly from the student portal. Fines for late returns can also be tracked and paid online, making library services accessible from anywhere.

    Under the leadership of the Polytechnic Librarian, we are committed to improving access to e-resources, creating a welcoming environment for study and research, and supporting the academic mission of the institution.

    — Dr Romanus Okechukwu Attama
    Polytechnic Librarian
