    About Us

    Federal Polytechnic Ugep

    Technology for Development and Excellence — established 2021

    Who We Are

    A young institution with an ambitious mission

    Federal Polytechnic Ugep is one of Nigeria's newest federal polytechnics, established in 2021 by the Federal Government of Nigeria to expand access to quality technical education in the South-South region.

    Located in Ugep, the headquarters of Yakurr Local Government Area of Cross River State, the institution sits on a 265-hectare permanent site, with a second campus along the Calabar–Ikom highway. Two-thirds of that land is dedicated to modern agricultural projects — a cornerstone of our mission.

    Our mandate is clear: train the middle-level technical manpower Nigeria needs to build a modern, self-reliant economy. We focus on the fields that matter most for the future — renewable energy and agricultural technology.

    Our Direction

    Vision, Mission & Philosophy

    Our Vision

    To develop an international standard Polytechnic dedicated to academic excellence and the acquisition of skills in Technology, Science and Management.

    Our Mission

    To train middle cadre manpower that will facilitate industrial development in Cross River State and Nigeria, through a team of enlightened, committed staff who motivate students to think outside the box.

    Our Philosophy

    Technical education is the engine of national development. We foster innovation, academic excellence, and practical skills that converge to produce graduates who transform society through knowledge and character.

    Our Locations

    Two Campuses, One Mission

    Main Campus

    Campus 1 — Ugep

    Located on the Ugep–Ediba road, this is our administrative and academic hub. Home to lecture halls, laboratories, the main library, and the Rector's office.

    Facilities:

    • Lecture halls & classrooms
    • Computer & science laboratories
    • Central library
    • Administrative block
    • Student hostels
    Agricultural Campus

    Campus 2 — Calabar/Ikom Highway

    Our agricultural and technology campus, spanning 65 hectares dedicated to practical farming, renewable energy projects, and hands-on technical training.

    Facilities:

    • Agricultural demonstration farms
    • Renewable energy workshop
    • Engineering workshops
    • SIWES training facilities
    • Research plots

    Our Journey

    Milestones

    2021

    Established by Federal Government

    Federal Polytechnic Ugep is officially established as part of the Federal Government's plan to expand technical education in the South-South region.

    2022

    First Academic Session

    The first set of students enrol in National Diploma programmes across science, engineering, and business departments.

    2024

    HND Programmes Launched

    Higher National Diploma programmes introduced in Software & Web Development and Networking & Cloud Computing.

    Today

    Growing, Innovating, Delivering

    With two operational campuses, 13 active programmes, and a growing student body, FPU is delivering on its mandate to train Nigeria's next generation of technical innovators.
