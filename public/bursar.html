    Welcome Message

    Transparent Financial Management

    Dr. Okoi Asuquo Effiom, a luminary in the field of accounting, has been appointed as the Bursar of Federal Polytechnic Ugep, a testament to his unwavering commitment to academic excellence and professional distinction.

    A seasoned professional with sterling academic and professional records, Dr. Effiom holds multiple qualifications, including a PhD in Accounting, a Master's degree in Accounting, a Bachelor's degree in Accounting, a Higher National Diploma (HND) in Accountancy, a Postgraduate Diploma in Education, and a National Diploma in Financial Studies.

    He is a Fellow of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria (FCA) and an Associate of the Chartered Institute of Taxation of Nigeria (ACTI), reflecting his mastery of accounting principles and practices.

    As Bursar, Dr. Effiom is poised to bring transformative leadership to Federal Polytechnic Ugep, leveraging his exceptional skills, technical expertise, and vision to drive financial management and administrative excellence.

    — Dr Okoi Asuquo Effiom
    Bursar, Federal Polytechnic Ugep
