    National Diploma

    ND Programmes

    11 two-year programmes across engineering, science, business, and technology

    Our National Diploma (ND) programmes run for two academic years (four semesters), followed by a mandatory period of Industrial Training (SIWES).

    All programmes are accredited by the National Board for Technical Education (NBTE).

    Academic Requirements

    • 5 O'Level credits (WAEC / NECO / NABTEB)
    • Must include English Language
    • Must include Mathematics
    • Not more than two sittings

    JAMB Requirements

    • UTME score of 100 and above
    • Chose FPU as first choice or
    • Applied for change of institution via JAMB
    • Minimum age: 16 years
